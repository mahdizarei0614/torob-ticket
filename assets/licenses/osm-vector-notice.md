# OpenStreetMap vector basemap notice

The optional hotel location map requests the official OpenStreetMap Foundation vector service on demand. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . The OSM contributor attribution remains visible in the map and supporting UI.

Official hosted styles are VersaTiles Colorful and Shadow, published with CC0 metadata: https://vector.openstreetmap.org/styles/shortbread/colorful.json and https://vector.openstreetmap.org/styles/shortbread/shadow.json . Their styles and assets are maintained by https://github.com/openstreetmap/vectortile-website and https://github.com/versatiles-org/versatiles-style . Retain upstream asset notices; the separately bundled MapLibre renderer license remains BSD-3-Clause.

Hosted tiles are community-funded and subject to https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/vector/ . Use ordinary on-demand browser caching and valid Referer; no bulk/offline downloads, prefetch, proxying or cache-bypass headers are provided by this app. Availability has no SLA. Basemap geography and local labels are real; the hotel and price inventory overlaid on it is fictional.
