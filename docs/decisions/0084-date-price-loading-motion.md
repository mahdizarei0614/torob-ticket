Date:2026-11-02. Owner request; presentation-only follow-up after decision0083.

structural skeleton geometry plus ordinary pending copy. Current valid pending
requests drive that state. Initial debounce counts as pending; invalid/unselected

accessibility-hidden; current values alone own accessible labels and actions.

query/leg/session price authority and date interaction. No invented curve,
minimum artificial delay, new package, persistent JS animation clock or renderer.

transitions and removes repeated visual animation. Finite exit cleanup must be
safe across rapid changes, unmount and development StrictMode.
